Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb).

The task: one sentence of at most 200 characters explaining the modern
internet to a time traveler from the year 1800, using no words coined
after their time — just a picture they could hold in their head.

The sentence (153 characters):

> Imagine every letter ever written, carried not by horse but by lightning through wires, so any man may read the whole world's words before his tea cools.

Every word in it — letter, horse, lightning, wire, tea — was already in
use long before 1800. The picture it builds is the post carried at the
speed of lightning: every letter ever written, readable by anyone,
faster than a cup of tea goes cold.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`, honouring the `ENTRY_OUTPUT`, `OUTPUT_DIR` and
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variables when set.
